# Limitations and deviations

This is a theorem audit, not a practical training reproduction. It establishes
existence but no constructive width, depth, optimization method, or rate. The
architecture's intersection definition `G_C=C_(1,C) intersect K` is also not a
directly enforceable training prescription.

The Lean theorem takes nonemptiness, lattice closure, and strict interpolation
of the paper-class as hypotheses. The paper's exact matrix-level realization
of those hypotheses remains outside the formal kernel. That is the material
remaining risk.
