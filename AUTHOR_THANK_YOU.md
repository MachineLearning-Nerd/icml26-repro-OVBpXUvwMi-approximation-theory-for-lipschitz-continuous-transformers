# Thank you to the authors

Thank you to Takashi Furuya, Davide Murari, and Carola-Bibiane Schönlieb for
developing and sharing this careful theoretical treatment of stability and
universal approximation in measure-valued in-context Transformers. The
paper's structure made it possible to separate layer stability, finite-depth
composition, abstract density arguments, and the remaining matrix-level
realization boundary.

This repository is an independent reproduction maintained by
[MachineLearning-Nerd](https://github.com/MachineLearning-Nerd). The note is
appreciation for the authors' work and does not imply author review,
endorsement, or agreement with the audit conclusions.
